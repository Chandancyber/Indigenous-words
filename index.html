Why this archive?

A word is never only a word.

Indigenous vocabulary often carries relationships that are difficult to translate into a single English equivalent. A word may encode a landscape, a social practice, a relationship with plants and animals, a ritual, or a form of knowledge.

This project presents those words with their contexts so that language can be approached as a cultural archive rather than a list of translations.
